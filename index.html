About me

Beyond Code. Into Engineering.

I'm a Software Service Engineer with a B.S. in Computer Science from the University of Wisconsin–Platteville. My work sits where software meets physical engineering systems: industrial automation, optical technology, machine integration and international technical support.

I specialize in supporting, installing, configuring, troubleshooting and upgrading software-driven industrial optical measurement and manufacturing systems, integrating software, hardware, databases, industrial communication protocols and machine control systems.

I support customers across North America, South America and Europe through remote troubleshooting, on-site installations, commissioning, software upgrades and training. I enjoy solving complex technical problems and learning new technologies.
